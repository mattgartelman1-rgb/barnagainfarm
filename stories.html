From the stand

Bubba's Lemonade

What started as a fun idea from Jess Gartelman and her boyfriend Frank has become one of the most refreshing parts of Barn Again Farm. They founded and inspired Bubba's Lemonade with a heart for summer fun, community connection and fresh, real ingredients.

Now Bubba's Lemonade is proudly operated by Matt Gartelman as part of the farm stand. Made fresh on-site and served ice cold, it's become a local favorite and a sweet symbol of what's possible when faith, family and hard work come together.
