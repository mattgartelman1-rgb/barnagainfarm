Our story

It started with one table and a big dream

Hi, I'm Matt Gartelman, founder of Barn Again Farm. I started this farm in 2023 when I was 11 years old with a simple table, a handful of produce, and a big dream: to honor God through hard work, generosity and farming.

I've always loved business, but more importantly, I love Jesus. After giving my life to Christ, my mission became clear: use this farm to share the gospel, bless my community and live boldly for the Lord. Whether I'm planting vegetables, selling honey or talking with customers at the stand, I do it all to point people to Jesus.

I believe God can use anyone, at any age, to make a difference. I'm just getting started.
